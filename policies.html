Shipping, cancellation & return policy

These customer-facing policies are now displayed during checkout. Please have the business owner review them before publishing and replace any terms that do not reflect actual operations.

Shipping & tracking

  • Orders are confirmed after checkout and packed before dispatch.
  • Courier name and tracking number are shown in the customer’s order tracking page after shipment.
  • Delivery dates are estimates and can vary by pincode, weather, holidays, and courier operations.

Cancellation

  • Customers can cancel an order free of charge until it is shipped.
  • Orders that have already been handed to the courier cannot be cancelled; customers may request a return if eligible.
  • For prepaid orders, approved refunds are processed to the original payment method after verification.

Returns

  • Return requests must be made within 7 days of delivery.
  • Products must be unused, unopened, sealed, and in their original packaging.
  • Opened food products, damaged products caused after delivery, and final-sale/perishable items are not eligible unless received damaged or incorrect.
  • Approved refunds or replacements are processed after the returned item is inspected.

Payments

  • Cash on Delivery is collected at delivery.
  • UPI payments are marked verification pending until the merchant verifies payment through its payment provider.
  • Never share UPI PIN or OTP with anyone.

Support

For an order, cancellation, or return request, contact Pinaki Farms with your order ID and registered mobile number.
